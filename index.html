What it remembers
Three kinds of memory. Yours, not ours.
Workspace-scoped: nothing crosses groups. You can pin, edit or wipe any of it.
— Facts

Things about your group

People, preferences, recurring details. The bot learns them as you talk and surfaces them when relevant.

  • Alice is allergic to peanuts.
  • WiFi password is stored under "Notes".
  • Trash day is Tuesday.
— Decisions

What you agreed on

Choices, plans, who's doing what — kept with the context. No more "wait, what did we decide?".

  • We're going to Lisbon, July 12–18.
  • Bob handles utilities. Alice handles groceries.
  • We split everything evenly.
— Schedule

What's coming up

Deadlines, recurring chores, follow-ups. The bot keeps the calendar — and pings the right person at the right moment.

  • Rent due on the 1st.
  • Take out the trash every Tuesday.
  • Follow up with the landlord in 2 weeks.
How it works
Three steps. Zero friction.
No app to download. No signup form. No onboarding wizard.
01

Add it to your group

One contact to add. The workspace is created automatically and only the people in the group can access it.

WhatsApp Telegram Discord
02

Talk like a person

No commands to learn. "Remind us Tuesday." "Note down the WiFi." "What did we decide about the trip?". The agent figures the rest out.

03

It remembers and acts

Plans, reminds, follows up — without being asked twice. Open the workspace anytime to see todos, calendar, notes, decisions and history.

Features
An agent. A memory. A real workspace.
Everything you say in the chat is captured, structured, recallable. Everything you do on the web syncs back.
M

Memory that compounds

Structured facts plus semantic search across every message. The agent finds what you said three months ago in seconds. You decide what stays, what gets pinned, what gets forgotten.

T

Todos that stick

Add from chat in plain language. Assign people, set deadlines, add priorities. Reply "done" to a task card to complete. List or kanban view on the web.

assignments deadlines priorities kanban
N

Notes that don't get buried

Pin important info from the chat. WiFi passwords, house rules, contact details — always findable, always editable in a real Markdown editor on the web.

markdown rich editor full-text search
F

Files in one place

Reply "save this" to any image or document. Stored, named, previewable on the web. No more scrolling back through months of messages to find the lease.

PDF preview image gallery drag & drop
W

Web search built-in

"Find a plumber near Lyon open Sunday." The agent searches the web, cites its sources, and pins the answer if you ask. Open backend (Brave today, self-hosted SearXNG soon).

sourced answers open backend
G

A workspace, not a dashboard

Memory, calendar, scheduled actions, todos, notes, files, history, settings. Member-only access via phone OTP — no passwords, no email signup. Mobile-friendly. Dark mode. Export your data anytime.
